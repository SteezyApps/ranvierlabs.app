Privacy Policy — Homeostasis

Ranvier Labs LLC, last updated 2026-11-23.

Homeostasis does not collect your data.

The app has no account system, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party SDKs that report usage, and no server of its own to send anything to. The app makes no network requests of its own. Buying a subscription, restoring one you already have, and checking whether your Apple ID is entitled to one all go through Apple's App Store on your device: Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether you are entitled. Nothing about you, and nothing about how you use the app, is sent to us or to anyone else — we have nowhere to send it and no way to read it.

WHAT THE APP STORES, AND WHERE

The app stores your simulation runs, your practice-question history and progress, a reminder preference, and a flag recording that you have seen the educational-use notice. All of it is written to your own device's local storage and stays there. None of it is transmitted anywhere.

YOUR CONTROL

"Download my data" writes everything the app has recorded into a single file you choose the destination for. "Delete my data" removes all of it from the device, including any file the app previously exported to its own storage. Deleting the app also removes it.

CHILDREN

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
