An educational simulation

Homeostasis is a teaching tool. It runs a physiology model so you can predict what a body will do and then watch it happen.

It is not a clinical tool. The scenarios, diseases and drug doses in it exist to teach mechanisms. Do not use it to make decisions about a real patient, and do not use it for diagnosis, treatment or dosing.
