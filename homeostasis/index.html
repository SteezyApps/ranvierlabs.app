Homeostasis

Run the body. Pass the exam.

A physiology engine in your pocket: change one thing, watch the whole body respond, then answer for it. Works offline. No account. Nothing leaves your phone.

Coming soon to iPhone

An educational simulation

Homeostasis is a teaching tool. It runs a physiology model so you can predict what a body will do and then watch it happen.

It is not a clinical tool. The scenarios, diseases and drug doses in it exist to teach mechanisms. Do not use it to make decisions about a real patient, and do not use it for diagnosis, treatment or dosing.

Practice questions marked Clinically unreviewed are complete, but a clinician has not yet counter-signed them. Each one names the published sources its answer rests on, so you can check it yourself.
