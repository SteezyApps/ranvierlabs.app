Turnpoint

Plan for the hard moments

Coming soon to iPhone

Turnpoint helps adults cut back on or quit a habit, or start a new one, with a plan built around the moments that are actually hard.

Most habit apps count days and send you back to zero the first time you slip. Turnpoint doesn't. A slip is information. Your progress only goes up.

WHAT YOU GET

  • A personal plan. Answer a few questions about when, where and why, and Turnpoint maps your hardest moments and suggests an if-then plan for each one.
  • Urge Now. One tap from any main screen: rate the urge, follow a guided urge-surfing or paced-breathing exercise, then see your own reason for changing and the plan you made.
  • Slips without shame. Log what happened, look at what you could change, update your plan. Your good days and urges ridden out stay exactly where they were.
  • Daily check-in. A few taps each evening so you and the app learn where your hard moments are.
  • An accountability partner. Invite a sponsor or someone you trust. You choose exactly what they can see, and one tap asks them for a check-in.
  • Start mode. Building a new habit instead? Pick a tiny version, attach it to something you already do, and check it off each day.

WITH A SUBSCRIPTION

  • AI coach. Talk things through any time. The coach is an AI, not a human, a therapist or a doctor, and it says so.
  • Reminders shortly before your tough times, and for your daily check-in.
  • Your full plan, editable at any time.
  • Insights into your triggers, your urges and your progress.

ALWAYS FREE

Urge Now, slip logging, check-ins, the help page and partner accounts are free and stay free.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

  • No ads and no tracking.
  • App lock with Face ID or fingerprint, and a discreet mode that keeps notifications neutral.
  • Export your data or delete your account from inside the app at any time.

IMPORTANT

Turnpoint is a support tool, not medical care, and is for adults aged 18 or older. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to a doctor about your health. If you are in crisis in the United States, call or text 988.
