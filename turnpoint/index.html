Quit weed with a real plan.

Not just a streak counter. Turnpoint builds a plan around your hardest moments, gives you something to do each day, and checks in right before the tough times.

Coming soon to iPhone. Android to follow.

Whether you want to stop smoking weed completely or cut back, Turnpoint starts where you are. There’s no judgment here, and a slip doesn’t send you back to zero. It’s information you use to sharpen the plan, and your progress only goes up.

How it works

  • A plan built for you. Answer a few questions about when, where and why you smoke. Turnpoint maps your hardest moments and suggests an if-then plan for each one, using if-then planning, a behavior-change technique backed by research.
  • Something to do every day. A quick evening check-in and small daily actions keep the plan moving, so quitting cannabis is something you work on, not just something you count.
  • Urge Now. One tap from any main screen: rate the urge, follow a guided urge-surfing or paced-breathing exercise, then see your own reason for changing and the plan you made.
  • Slips without shame. Log what happened, look at what you could change, and update your if-then plan for that moment. Your good days stay exactly where they were.
  • Someone in your corner. Invite a sponsor or someone you trust. You choose exactly what they can see, and one tap asks them for a check-in.

With a subscription: a plan that shows up for you

  • Reminders timed to your hard moments. Turnpoint learns when your urges usually hit and checks in just before, so you have your plan in hand when it counts, not after.
  • A plan you sharpen as you go. After a slip, Turnpoint helps you update your if-then plan for that moment. Edit any part, any time.
  • See your patterns. Insights into your triggers, your urges and how far you’ve come, so you know what’s working.
  • An AI coach, any time. Talk it through at 2 a.m. or before a tough night out. It’s an AI, not a person or a therapist, and it says so.

Always free

Urge Now, slip logging, check-ins, the help page and partner accounts are free and stay free.

Private by design

  • No ads and no tracking.
  • App lock with Face ID or fingerprint, and a discreet mode that keeps notifications neutral.
  • Export your data or delete your account from inside the app at any time.

More habits are coming

Turnpoint launches for quitting weed first. The same approach works for any habit you want to stop or start, and more are on the way. Building a new habit instead? Start mode lets you pick a tiny version, attach it to something you already do, and check it off each day.
