Turnpoint Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Turnpoint is published by Ranvier Labs LLC ("we"). Turnpoint is for adults aged 18 or older. This policy says what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Email address and sign-in identifier (from email sign-up, Sign in with Apple or Google)To create and secure your account
Date of birthTo confirm you are 18 or older
What you enter about your habit: your onboarding answers, your reason for changing, your if-then plans, urge logs, slip logs, check-ins, and what you typically spentTo build your plan, show your progress and, if you subscribe, time your reminders and inform the coach
Messages you send to the AI coach, and its repliesTo provide the coach. Kept for 90 days, then deleted.
The display name you choose for a partner, your sharing choices, and "reach out" requestsTo provide the accountability partner feature
A push notification address for your deviceTo deliver a partner's request for a check-in
Subscription status and purchase historyTo unlock paid features

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising identifier. The app contains no advertising and no tracking, and we do not sell your data.

Information about substance use is sensitive. We use it only to provide the app to you.

Who we share it with

CompanyWhat they receiveWhy
SupabaseYour account and everything stored in the appHosting and database
AnthropicYour coach messages and a short summary of your plan and recent check-ins, only if you agree in the app before using the coachTo generate the coach's replies
RevenueCat, Apple and GoogleAn account identifier and your purchasesTo process and verify subscriptions
Expo, Apple and GoogleYour device's push address and the notification textTo deliver push notifications

Your accountability partner sees only the categories you switch on, never your notes, reflections, reasons or coach messages.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Where reminders are kept

Reminders are scheduled on your own phone. In discreet mode their text is neutral and does not mention the habit.

How long we keep it

Coach messages are deleted after 90 days. Everything else is kept until you delete it or delete your account.

Your choices

  • Export your data in the app: Settings → Export my data.
  • Delete your account and all its data in the app: Settings → Delete my account. You can also ask at this page.
  • Withdraw from the coach by not using it; its messages are deleted after 90 days or with your account.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights to access, correct or delete your data. Contact us to use them.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access rules in the database let each person read only their own records. You can add app lock with Face ID or fingerprint.

Children

Turnpoint is not for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Not medical care

Turnpoint is a support tool. It is not medical care and the coach is an AI, not a clinician.

Changes and contact

We will post changes here and update the date above. Questions: support@ranvierlabs.app.
