Delete your Turnpoint account

Turnpoint is published by Ranvier Labs LLC.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Turnpoint and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete my account.
  3. Confirm with Delete everything.

Without the app

Email support@ranvierlabs.app from the address your account uses, with the subject "Delete my Turnpoint account". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account and everything in it: your email address and date of birth, your plan, urge logs, slip logs, check-ins, coach messages, partner connections, device push address and subscription record. Nothing is kept afterwards, except where the law requires us to keep a record (for example, of a purchase).

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
